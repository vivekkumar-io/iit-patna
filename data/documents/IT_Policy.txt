Oeeggis Corporation - IT Security Policy

1. Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems.

2. Device Usage
Company-issued laptops must have approved antivirus software installed.
Personal devices may not access internal systems without IT approval.

3. Data Classification
Confidential data must not be shared via personal email or unapproved cloud services.
All customer data is classified as confidential.

4. Software Installation
Only software from the approved catalog may be installed on company devices.
Requests for new software go through the IT service desk.

5. Incident Reporting
Security incidents must be reported to security@oeeggis.com within 1 hour of discovery.
Do not attempt to investigate security breaches independently.

6. Remote Access
VPN is required when accessing company resources from outside the office network.
VPN credentials are personal and must not be shared.

7. Email and Phishing
Employees must complete annual security awareness training.
Suspected phishing emails should be reported using the "Report Phishing" button.
