Oeeggis Corporation - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How do I request annual leave?
A: Submit your request through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance.

Q: Can I work from home?
A: Yes, up to 2 days per week with your manager's approval.

Q: When does health insurance start?
A: Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the self-service portal or contact the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@oeeggis.com.

Q: What is the dress code?
A: Business casual. Client-facing roles may require business formal attire.

Q: How many sick days do I get?
A: 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is needed for more than 3 consecutive days.

Q: Can I carry forward unused leave?
A: Yes, up to 5 days can be carried forward and must be used by March 31 of the following year.

Q: How do I submit travel expenses?
A: Submit expense reports within 14 days of your trip via the Finance portal with receipts for expenses over $25.

Q: Is there an employee assistance program?
A: Yes, free confidential counseling is available 24/7 through the EAP.

Q: What is the learning budget?
A: $1,500 per year for courses, certifications, and conferences.
