Oeeggis Corporation - Benefits Documentation

1. Health Insurance
Company provides comprehensive health coverage for employees and dependents.
Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

2. Dental and Vision
Dental and vision plans are included at no additional cost for employees.
Dependent coverage is available at subsidized rates.

3. Retirement Plan
401(k) plan with 4% company match after 1 year of service.
Employees may contribute up to IRS limits.

4. Life Insurance
Basic life insurance equal to 1x annual salary is provided at no cost.
Supplemental life insurance is available through payroll deduction.

5. Wellness Program
Annual wellness stipend of $500 for gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellness apps.
On-site health screenings are offered quarterly.

6. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
Free confidential counseling for employees and family members.
Available 24/7 via phone or online portal.

7. Professional Development
Annual learning budget of $1,500 per employee for courses, certifications, and conferences.
