Oeeggis Corporation - Travel Policy

1. Pre-Approval
All business travel must be pre-approved by the direct manager and Finance for trips exceeding $2,000.

2. Booking
Flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal.
Economy class is standard for domestic travel; business class requires VP approval for international flights over 8 hours.

3. Daily Allowances
Domestic travel per diem: $75/day for meals and incidentals.
International travel per diem varies by destination (see Finance portal).

4. Expense Submission
Expense reports must be submitted within 14 days of trip completion.
Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.

5. Personal Travel
Combining personal and business travel is permitted with prior approval.
Personal portions of mixed trips are not reimbursable.

6. Ground Transportation
Rental cars require manager approval.
Ride-sharing services are preferred for local travel.

7. Cancellation
Travel insurance is recommended for international trips.
Cancellation fees may be reimbursed if the trip was cancelled for business reasons.

8. Woek From HOME (WFH)
WFH is allowd only for non metro city in india
